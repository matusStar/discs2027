## Travel
The best way to travel around Ireland is to use a TFI Leap Card, which can be used on public transport services:
- Bus Éireann
- Dublin Bus
- Go‑Ahead Ireland
- TFI Luas
- DART and commuter rail services within the Dublin area
A Leap Card can be purchased at SPAR or other TFI agents. More information available on [https://about.leapcard.ie/](https://about.leapcard.ie/). 

### Getting to TCD
TCD is centrally located and can be reached by DART, Luas or bus.

### From Dublin Airport:
There are several bus routes into Dublin city centre from the airport.
- [Aircoach](https://www.aircoach.ie/?c=1795433245) has stops at Zone 2 outside Terminal 1 and Zone 20 outside Terminal 2. 
- The [Dublin Express](https://www.dublinexpress.ie/dublin-city/dublin-airport-to-dublin-city) buses run from Zone 1 outside Terminal 1 and Zone 21 outside Terminal 2. 
- TFI bus routes (payment with exact change or Leap Card): Routes 16 and 41 run from Zone 15 outside Terminal 1. Routes 19 and 24 run from Zone 11 outside Terminal 1.

Please refer to the airport [bus stop map](https://www.dublinairport.com/docs/default-source/maps/2023-transport-map.pdf?sfvrsn=d5dcf34f_2) for more details.
- Taxi: Taxis are readily available just outside the airport Terminal 1.
